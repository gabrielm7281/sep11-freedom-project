# Entry 6
##### 6/1/26

### What Have I Done So Far?
In between the last blog and this finaly blog, I have finalized the project and presented it as well as gotten the final grade. Coding wise I havent really done much. I redid the hitboxes for the platforms since they were way too big previously and I attempted to add in another enemy but it didnt go too well and I lost motivation pretty quickly. However, every basic function works and it is playable. To show off the project: we first had to present slides about our process and challenges to the class. This lasted for a week or two and it was just to see how everyones project turned out. The second way we did it was the SEP Expo. This was slightly more serious as kids from other grades could come and look at our projects. We had to give "elevator pitches" to them so that they could take notes. There were also judges roaming around who graded us on our presentations and the product. 

The biggest takeaways from both the expo and the presentations were to shorten it. My presentation wasnt timed but some people said it went on for a long time. My elevator pitch to the judges also went on for too long and they didnt have time to ask questions. I think taking out the useless information would have been much better for me instead of including the context. My other takeaway was to increase my confidence in myself. I am not the most passionate about my game since it didn't go very well and I think it might of showed in the expo. Nobody I talked to seemed intrested for very long and I think if I increased my confidence on the project, I could have been a little more excited which might of kept people around for longer. 

### Skills I Learned
I think I have definitley improved my presentation skills. In the feedback from Mr. Mueller, he says I have improved my speaking and eye contact by a lot compared to last year. I think this is very important, and it was also emphaisized later that the soft skills that I get from SEP can be used in the future. I think this is one of those skills.

### EDP
The last step of the Engineering Design Process is to communicate the results. This is shown through the presentations that I have done which show off my game (communication). All skills and feedback also included communication which further strengthens my position in the EDP.
